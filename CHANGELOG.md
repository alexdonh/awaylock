# Changelog

## Unreleased

First public release.

- Locks your Mac when your chosen Bluetooth device has been away for a few seconds, or the moment its signal is lost.
- Keeps the Mac awake and unlocked while the device is near.
- Works with Apple Watch and iPhone on the same iCloud account, and with bands that advertise with a fixed address.
- "Set from where I am now" calibration and a live signal needle in Settings.
- Optional auto-unlock when you come back (off by default; uses your Keychain and Accessibility).
- Pause for 1 hour, Lock Now, start at login.
- Automatic updates that install only while your device is near.
- Available in English, Vietnamese, Simplified Chinese, Japanese, Korean, Spanish, French and German.
